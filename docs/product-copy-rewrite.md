# Ecommerce Better Buys: Product Titles & Descriptions Rewrite

**Scope:** all 31 products on the Shop page, grouped by category.
**Voice:** friendly, straightforward, expert.

**How to use this:**
- Paste the **New title** into the product name.
- Paste the **Short description** into the short description box (the text beside the photo).
- Paste the **Bullets** at the top of the long description.
- Keep the original brand name in the product's brand or attribute field so people searching for it can still find the item.

**Please verify before publishing:** I wrote every claim from your current titles only (sizes, pack counts, BPA-free, 13ft drop protection, and so on). I haven't seen your supplier specs. Check anything about materials, dimensions, compatibility and warranty against the supplier listing. Never publish a claim you can't back up.

## Store-Wide Quick Wins
1. **Add a photo for every product** and include at least one lifestyle or in-use image where you can.
2. **Pack-vs-single clarity:** products 11 and 12 (spice jars) and 15 and 16 (pet brushes) compete with each other. Remove duplicates or position them as "good / better."
3. **Bundles:** build "Pet Grooming Kit," "Phone Glow-Up" (case plus charms) and "Soap Station" sets at 10% off.
4. **Free-shipping threshold:** set it above your average order (around $35 to $40) to raise basket size.
5. **Reviews:** turn on WooCommerce reviews and email buyers 7 days after delivery.
6. **Category descriptions:** add 80 to 120 words per category with natural keywords such as "dog toys," "iPhone cases" and "garden tools."
